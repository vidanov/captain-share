# Code of conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be kind and constructive, assume good intent, welcome people of every
background and experience level, and keep discussions about the work. Harassment,
insults, and publishing others' private information are not accepted.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainer,
[@vidanov](https://github.com/vidanov), through a GitHub direct contact or a
private security advisory if the matter is sensitive. Reports are handled
confidentially. The maintainer may remove comments, commits or contributions that
do not follow this code, and may restrict participation.
